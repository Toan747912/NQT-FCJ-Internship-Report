### Why I attended

I joined FCAJ Community Day to:

- Listen to real experiences from the First Cloud Journey / AWS community.
- Learn how to **present and share** ideas and teamwork under time pressure.
- Pick up a **practical cloud learning roadmap** to apply during my internship.

---

Based on the sharing sessions at **FCAJ Community Day**, below is a summary of the key content combined with my own experience as an attendee:

### 1. A practical perspective from *The Ballers* (Huynh An Khuong, Mai Quoc Anh, Nguyen Tran Minh Quan)

Their sharing was highly hands-on, focused on the experience of building a project "in the trenches" under crunch time. Key takeaways included:

- **Rapid iteration mindset:** embracing trial and error and continuous learning throughout development.
- **Team skills:** clear task division combined with constant communication was the key to shipping successfully under a tight deadline.
- **Handling pressure:** a realistic account of balancing output, bug fixes, and personal well-being (sleep loss, exhaustion) to finish the project.

### 2. A practical learning roadmap (Huynh Thai Linh)

The speaker proposed a 3-phase model to build a sustainable cloud mindset, avoiding the trap of jumping straight into using services without a proper foundation:

| Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| **Phase 1 – Mindset & Architecture** | Build a foundation through **AWS Cloud Quest** (suited to Cloud Practitioner level) |
| **Phase 2 – Code & Fast Testing** | Practice with open-source tools such as **Floci** to streamline the testing workflow |
| **Phase 3 – Real-world Deployment** | Bring the application into a real AWS environment |

---

### 3. Applying this to my internship and personal project

Combining the real-world experience with the roadmap above gives me a solid basis to apply to my internship and report:

- **URL Shortener project:** I can map this roadmap onto my own project — grasp the architecture first, then implement the code on serverless services (**Lambda, DynamoDB, S3**), and finally operate it on real AWS.
- **Skill development:** carry forward the rapid-iteration mindset and practice explaining architecture concisely, the way the speakers did.

### Personal experience

As an **attendee**, I followed two consecutive sharing sessions on Floor 26 of Bitexco: one grounded in real build-under-pressure experience, the other a structured learning roadmap. It was a fairly well-rounded combination of **soft skills** (communication, teamwork) and a **solid technical roadmap** (cloud), giving me clearer direction for my internship at FCAJ/AWS.

> Overall, FCAJ Community Day helped me learn how to communicate experience while also giving me a practical cloud learning frame to apply directly to my serverless project and internship report.
