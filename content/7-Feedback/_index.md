Below are my honest, personal impressions after joining **First Cloud Journey – AWS FCJ HCM**, shared in the hope that they are useful feedback for the FCJ team.

### Overall Evaluation

**1. Working Environment**  
The environment is generally open and easy to approach, with a weekly roadmap that made it clear what to focus on. It can still feel a bit self-paced/individual at times, and a few more group or bonding activities would help interns connect with each other, not just with the material.

**2. Support from the Admin Team**  
The admin team was willing to explain things and pointed me toward researching first before asking for answers, which nudged me toward self-learning. Response time on some questions varied depending on how busy things were, which is understandable but worth noting.

**3. Relevance of Work to Academic Major**  
The topics covered — IAM, VPC, EC2, serverless, containers, AI/ML — line up reasonably well with an IT background. Several of them went beyond what university courses typically cover, which was useful, though the depth on any single topic was naturally limited by the 12-week timeframe.

**4. Learning & Skill Development Opportunities**  
Over 12 weeks I picked up practical AWS Console/CLI skills, worked through Cloud Journey and AWS Study Group labs, and practiced debugging common issues. The labs are structured around individual completion, so there was less exposure to how this kind of work happens in a team setting.

**5. Company Culture & Team Spirit**  
People were generally respectful and willing to share knowledge when someone got stuck. As an intern the interaction was mostly task-focused, so I did not get a strong sense of the broader team culture beyond the labs themselves.

**6. Internship Policies / Benefits**  
The lab path and materials are reasonably clear, and Cloud Credits were provided for hands-on practice. Study time was flexible, which helped with balancing self-study outside scheduled sessions.

---

### Additional Questions

- **What was most satisfying during the internship?**  
  Getting to practice directly on AWS rather than only reading about it, and working through debugging issues (Security Group, Route Table, IAM, Lambda) on my own.

- **What should be improved for future interns?**  
  Introducing some architecture-review discussion (Well-Architected) earlier would help interns understand the reasoning behind a design, not just how to finish the lab steps.

- **Would you recommend this internship to a friend? Why?**  
  With some reservations, yes — mainly for students who are comfortable with a lot of self-directed learning. The roadmap and lab materials are solid, but expect the guidance to be lighter-touch rather than closely mentored.

---

### Suggestions & Expectations

- **Suggestions to improve the internship experience:**  
  - Short sharing sessions every 2–3 weeks so interns can present what they've built and get feedback from peers, not just admins.  
  - More guidance on AWS certification study paths after the program ends.

- **Would you like to continue this program in the future?**  
  I am open to staying connected with the FCJ / AWS Study Group community to keep learning, though I would want to see how the earlier suggestions play out.

- **Other comments:**  
  Thanks to First Cloud Journey and the admin team for the support over these 12 weeks. The program gave me a reasonable starting point in AWS, with some clear areas — deeper mentoring, more team-based work — that could still be strengthened for future cohorts.
