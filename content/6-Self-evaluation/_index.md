Over the course of my internship at **First Cloud Journey – AWS FCJ HCM** from **17/04/2026** to **31/07/2026**, I went from having almost no cloud background to being able to build and run a system on AWS on my own. Getting there took a fair amount of trial and error, working through labs and official documentation rather than starting from a strong foundation.

The most concrete outcome is a **serverless URL Shortener** (S3, Lambda Function URL, DynamoDB, CloudWatch, SNS) that I deployed myself, along with a **proposal** sketching out next steps such as CI/CD, monitoring, and cost controls. These reflect what I was able to accomplish within the scope and time of the internship — not a production-ready system.

Looking back, I still have clear limitations, particularly in architecture design depth and confidence when presenting, so I see this more as a starting point to keep building on than a finished result.

### Strengths

* **Persistence with labs and docs:** didn't give up on errors, kept re-reading AWS documentation and trying different approaches before asking for help.
* **Followed through on commitments:** kept pace with the 12-week schedule and submitted labs and reports on time.
* **Open to feedback:** took admin feedback seriously and adjusted my approach in the following weeks.
* **Careful with written work:** tried to keep the report clear and complete in both languages.

### Needs Improvement

* My understanding of **AWS architecture** is still fairly basic and needs more time to grasp real-world trade-offs.
* Unfamiliar or complex errors still take me a while to work through before I find the right fix.
* **Verbal presentation** and live technical discussion are still areas I'm not confident in.
* **Technical English** sometimes slows down how quickly I can read documentation.
* I have limited experience with **team collaboration** on a real project — most of my work so far has been individual.
